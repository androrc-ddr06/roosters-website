Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you book

What areas do you serve? +

We're based in Vacaville, CA and bring our rolling BBQ to events across all of Northern California — including Fairfield, Dixon, Davis, Napa, Sacramento, and the greater Solano County area. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

Do you cook the food on-site? +

Yes. Everything is cooked fresh over live fire right at your event. Carne asada, al pastor, brisket, tri-tip, and grilled chicken are made on-site and served hot — never pre-made and reheated.

What kinds of events do you cater? +

Weddings, company picnics, birthdays, graduations, quinceañeras, corporate events, and backyard parties — from intimate gatherings to events of 150+ guests. Tell us what you're planning and we'll build a menu to fit.

What's included in a package? +

Every package includes your choice of proteins and sides plus full setup, service, and cleanup. We handle the rig, the cooking, and the serving so you can enjoy your own event. See the packages page for details.

Is there a minimum order? +

Package pricing is per person, and minimum headcounts may apply depending on your event. À la carte orders have a $250 minimum. Reach out and we'll tailor a quote to your group size.

How far in advance should I book? +

As early as you can — popular dates, especially summer weekends, fill up fast. Once you submit an inquiry we'll confirm availability within 24–48 hours.

Can you accommodate vegetarians or dietary needs? +

Yes — grilled veggies can be added to any taco package for +$3 per person, and we offer several vegetarian-friendly sides like mac & cheese, salads, and pasta. Let us know about any dietary needs when you book and we'll work them in.

How do I get a quote? +

Send us your date and guest count through the chat on our booking page — or just call or text — and Ruben will follow up within 24–48 hours with availability and a custom quote.

Ready to Fire Up Your Event?

Send us your event details by chat, call, or text and we'll get back to you within 24–48 hours with a custom quote.
